DEHRADUN GIS DASHBOARD - INCOMING DATA FOLDER
========================================================================

Place your new layer data files here to import them into the dashboard:

Supported File Types:
  - GeoJSON (.geojson, .json): Vector features, boundaries, networks, sensors
  - ESRI Shapefile (.zip): Zipped shapefiles containing .shp, .shx, .dbf, .prj
  - GeoTIFF (.tif, .tiff) or PNG (.png): Raster heatmaps, terrain, flood grids
  - CSV (.csv): Point data with Latitude & Longitude columns

How to Use:
  1. Drop your files into this folder (`data/incoming/`).
  2. Open the Layer Studio in your browser (`admin.html`).
  3. Your files will appear in the "Incoming Data Files" section.
  4. Click "Add to Dashboard", customize visuals (colors, opacity), and click "Save & Sync".
  5. Your main dashboard (`index.html`) will instantly display the new layers!
